    Move

    Step per click

    Each arrow click moves the mount one step at the goto slew speed. N/S turn the Dec axis, E/W turn the RA axis (15° = 1 hour of RA). An E/W move across the meridian may flip the mount to the other side of the pier.

    Goto slew speed

    %

    Speeds marked ≈ are estimates: this firmware reports its speed rounded to 0.1 °/s.

    The speed is stored in the mount. It stays while the mount is powered, also when you unplug USB and connect ASIAIR. Whether it survives switching the mount off depends on its firmware: power-cycle the mount and check here.
